SapioSpend Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

SapioSpend ("the App") is a personal budgeting and expense-management application developed by Eleazar Cole-Showers.

Information We Collect

SapioSpend does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

The budgeting information you enter into SapioSpend, including budgets, expenses, event information, and related financial planning information, is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database.

This information is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into SapioSpend is used solely to provide the application's budgeting and expense-management functionality.

Data Sharing

SapioSpend does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. There are currently no advertising, analytics, or external data-collection services used by SapioSpend.

Data Security

Your SapioSpend data is stored locally on your Android device. We do not transmit your budgeting information to external servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it through the application or remove the application's data or uninstall the application. We do not retain copies of your data on external servers.

Children's Privacy

SapioSpend is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality or data practices of SapioSpend change. Any updated version will be made available at this same location, with the "Last updated" date changed accordingly.
